GOVERNMENT OF ARUNACHAL PRADESH: ITANAGAR

Dated Itanagar, the {{selectedQuarter.startDate | date:"dd/MM/yyyy"}}



RENEWAL OF ALLOTMENT ORDER

      The Government Quarter No {{selectedQuarter.qrtr_no}} /Type {{selectedQuarter.qrtr_type}} located at {{selectedQuarter.sctr_name}} Sector, {{selectedQuarter.loc_name}} allotted to Mr./ Ms {{selectedQuarter.f_name}} {{selectedQuarter.l_name}} Designation {{selectedQuarter.designation}} Department {{selectedQuarter.dept_name}}, is hereby renewed w.e.f {{selectedQuarter.startDate | date:"dd/MM/yyyy"}} for a further period of (3) three years on the following terms & conditions-
  • The allottee shall on receipt of this order, submit acceptance of the allotment within 7 (Seven) days to the allotting authority. However, in case of Renewal of Allotment Order this is not applicable.
  • The allottee of the quarter shall also intimate the actual date of occupation to the concerned Executive Engineer of the PWD / PHE /Power Department and his/her controlling Officer.
  • Similarly, while vacating the quarters on transfer / superannuation the allottee shall intimate the actual date of vacation to all concerned department as under(2) above.
  • The allottee shall submit the list of inventories of the quarter on occupying/vacating the quarter and seek ‘NO DUES CERTIFICATE’ from this office.
  • The allottee shall occupy the house subject to vacation by the former allottee and shall not take law into own hand in vacating the former allottee but to report the authority for taking necessary proceedings.
  • In case of advance allotment against superannuation, the allottee shall occupy the quarters only if the occupant vacates the quarters on expiry of the validity of his / her allotment including the retention period.
  • This allotment order is valid only for a period of three years from the date of issue. The allottee shall apply for renewal of the allotment order 1 month before expiry of the order failing which the quarter shall be allotted to other deserving applicants.
  • The allottee shall not modify, extend or demolish any part of quarter or its premises without permission from the allotting authority.
  • It shall be the responsibility of the allottee to report any kind of encroachments to allotting authority.
  • If the allottee commits encroachment of the quarter himself/herself, the allotment shall be cancelled forthwith.
  • If the allottee indulge in any kind of encroachments, demolition or encircle quarters and its premises by himself/herself or by any of his/her family members, such allottee shall not be issued ‘No Objection Certificate’ for obtaining ‘Last Payment Certificate (LPC)’ and ‘Pension Certificate’ during transfer or retirement.
  • Failure to abide by the above terms and conditions will be liable to be prosecuted as per law.

Sd/-

Director (Housing)
